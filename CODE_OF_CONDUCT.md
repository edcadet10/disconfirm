# Code of Conduct

`disconfirm` is committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive open-source
community. Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to follow this
code, regardless of role or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, direct, and respectful.
- Challenge claims and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and differing experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or unwanted personal attention.
- Discriminatory, sexualized, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, commits, and
other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's content-reporting tools. For a
confidential project report, use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/edcadet10/disconfirm/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `[Conduct]`. Do not report conduct concerns publicly.

Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy, review available context, and
respond proportionately. Responses may include a private correction, warning,
content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Knowingly false
or retaliatory reports are themselves violations. When safety and privacy allow,
maintainers will explain moderation decisions to the affected people.
